# Blacksburg Entertainment
From scenic trails to local bars, entertainment is scattered all across Blacksburg and the local area. Whether or not you're searching for a vibrant, busy venue or a quiet, scenic walk, there's enough in Blacksburg to keep anyone active and entertained for the years many people spend here.

## Milk Parlor

| --- | --- |
| Public Consensus | 4.3/5 |
| Price Range | $10-$20 |
| Location | 211 B Draper Rd NW, Blacksburg, VA| 
| Popularity | 5/10 | 
| Overall Rating | 7/10 | 

The Milk Parlor is characterized by the fat cats painted on the outside brick, live music, and sticky floors that don't let you shake the dive bar vibe the second you walk in. While they don’t have an extensive food menu, they’re known for their gooey grilled cheeses and mozzarella sticks. They have line dancing nights and are typically used as a venue for live music on busier nights.

## Lyric Theatre

| :---: | :---: |
| Public Consensus | 4.8/5 |
| Price Range | $0-$10 |
| Location | 135 College Ave, Blacksburg, VA | 
| Popularity | 7/10 | 
| Overall Rating | 8/10 | 

The Lyric is a nonprofit historic theatre built in the 1930s and has been preserved to show both mainstream and indie movies. They show movies at a cheap price compared to AMC and Regal, so they’re a great alternative for those looking to catch a film for half the price.

## Gillie's - Vegetarian Restaurant

| :---: | :---: |
| Public Consensus | 4.5/5 |
| Price Range | $10-$20 |
| Location | 153 College Ave, Blacksburg, VA | 
| Popularity | 7/10 | 
| Overall Rating | 8/10 | 

Gillie’s is a vegetarian restaurant that serves breakfast, lunch, and dinner, while also serving late night breakfast on certain days. They typically host local bands and musicians, which draws a large late-night crowd on weekends. They also have board games available, which makes for a lively and loud atmosphere during its peak hours.

## Coral Bank Hollow 

| :---: | :---: |
| Public Consensus | 4.5/5 |
| Price Range | Varied |
| Location | @coralbankhollow on Instagram | 
| Popularity | 7/10 | 
| Overall Rating | 8/10 | 

A local garage band in Blacksburg, VA, Coral Bank Hollow is composed of 5 members who play at local venues, events, and locations across Virginia. Most performances are announced through Instagram, so that’s the most convenient way to see them live. Because they classify themselves as a garage band, their impromptu performances are part of their charm.

## Cascades

| :---: | :---: |
| Public Consensus | 4.9/5 |
| Price Range | Free |
| Location | 2068 Cascade Dr, Pembroke, Virginia |
| Popularity | 9/10 | 
| Overall Rating | 9/10 | 

A beautiful 4-mile trail following a stream located 45 minutes away from campus, the Cascades is a Virginia Tech student favorite. When students want to get away from the hustle and bustle of school life, the trails become very busy and sometimes overwhelming. To avoid big crowds, consider going on early morning or late afternoon hikes.

## Huckleberry Trail

| :---: | :---: |
| Public Consensus | 5/5 |
| Price Range | Free |
| Location | Starts at the Blacksburg Public Library |
| Popularity | 8/10 | 
| Overall Rating | 9/10 | 

For those wanting a multipurpose trail for walking, running, or biking, Huckleberry Trail’s 15-mile long walkway is a beautiful location in Blacksburg and Christiansburg. Throughout the trail, there are multiple points of access, so users can hop on and off the trail easily.

## Cambria Station Antiques

| :---: | :---: |
| Public Consensus | 4.7/5 |
| Price Range | $10-$100+ |
| Location | 596 Depot St NE, Christiansburg, VA |
| Popularity | 7/10 | 
| Overall Rating | 8/10 | 

Cambria Station Antiques provides 3 floors of unique finds, handmade furniture, and valuable items. While you can definitely find items that are out of your budget, there’s such a wide selection of antiques that anyone could find something in their price range. They also have regular sales on items, which means that you could potentially score an item for less than what it’s valued for.
