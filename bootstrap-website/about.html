About Me

All About Max

Hey, I'm Max, and this is my website. This site primarily functions as a repository for all of the graphic design projects I've made thus far, as well as any projects I will make in the future.

As I mentioned on the home page, I've been making art for most of my life. Ever since I was a kid, hobbies like writing and drawing captivated me; I spent hours upon hours creating any dumb little thing that came to mind, even if the end result wasn't very good. To me, that didn't matter, just the process of making art itself was all I needed to have a good time. As I grew older, I honed my skills, branching out into different artistic mediums as I did, eventually leading me to graphic design.

Outside of practicing graphic design, art, and writing, I'm also into playing video games, listening to music, and watching anime and film. Some of my favorite films include: Reservoir Dogs, Trainspotting, Hot Fuzz, The Big Lebowski, and High Fidelity (which is my favorite out of all of them). I'd highly recommend checking them out if you can!

While you're here, I hope you enjoy all the work I've done so far. Once new graphic design projects are completed, more pieces will be added to the gallery, so make sure to check back for updates if my work interests you. Thanks for visiting!
