About Me

All About Max

Hey, I'm Max, and this is my website. Mainly, this site functions as a repository for all of the graphic design projects I've made thus far, and any projects I will make in the future.

Like I mentioned before, I've been doing art for most of my life. Ever since I was a kid, hobbies like writing and drawing came naturally to me, as I spent hours upon hours creating any dumb little thing that came to mind. As I grew older, I honed these skills, branching out into different artistic mediums as I did, eventually leading me to graphic design.

Outside of graphic design and writing, I'm also into video games, anime, and film. Some of my favorite films include: Reservoir Dogs, Trainspotting, Hot Fuzz, The Big Lebowski, and High Fidelity (which is my favorite out of all of them). I'd highly recommend checking them out if you can!
