My Graphic Design Gallery

SotCJ Logo

This is main logo I designed for Sins of the Concrete Jungle, a fiction series that I've been working on since the start of my Freshman year of college.

I use a lot of bold, minimalistic shapes and colors in my designs, and this is no exception. I wanted to create something that embodied the feeling of my work while also keeping things simple, allowing the contrast present in the piece to stand out.

While figuring out the design, I mainly looked at the typography work on the anime Neon Genesis Evangelion for inspiration. Mainly, I really liked how the font looked in the show's episode title cards, so I tried my best to replicate it here, something I did very well.

SotCJ Eyecatcher

This is another design I did for my series, as mentioned above. This time, I wanted to capture a certain tone of uneasiness and surveillance while using the book's setting, New York City, as a backdrop.

I'm sure it's easy to tell, considering the only bit of typography in the design, but the Radiohead song of the same name was a big inspiration for this piece. The cold, electronic sound of the song lent itself incredibly well to the ideas I wanted to convey, so it was looping in the background for most of the time that I worked on it.

It was hard managing all the objects when I was limited to Google Slides as my main graphic design program, but I still think it turned out alright.

Konran Dreams Logo

This design is the finalized rough draft of a logo I'm working on for a friend's Dungeons and Dragons Campaign. In early March, 2026, I got a message from him asking me if I wouldn't mind making a logo for a faction in his campaign, and, because I needed an excuse to get back into graphic design, I got to work on it immediately.

Again, because this design is rough, things are obviously subject to change. As it is right now, however, I wanted give off a space-y sort of feeling, which is mainly why the comet is there. Since the faction is mostly comprised of people from another world, I figured this made this.

Unlike the designs above, I didn't have a direct inspiration for this piece. Since I was getting back into the hobby at the time, I wanted to give myself some room to experiment.

ECC Flyer

During the fall semester of my Senior year at Virginia Tech, I was part of a class called Words and Pictures that made communications for the English department.

This design was made towards the start of the semester, promoting the then-upcoming English Career Connections event. Every student in the class had to make one, so there ended up being about 10 poster designs the head of the event had to choose from.

While my design wasn't chosen, it was still well received, much to my relief. Again, I tried to incorporate the high-contrast minimalism found in my other designs, though, because I was working with a limited color palette, it was a little harder than some of the personal work I had done.

Podcast Graphic

This design is another I made for the Words and Pictures class I took, going along with an episode of the "English Podcast" that I had to create that focused on the often-overlooked art of screenwriting.

Because I wanted the podcast to be the main focus of this project, I kept the design minimal, even more so than usual. I didn't want it to be bright or overbearing, so I opted for something a little more monochrome.

I wanted to make it visually appealing, of course, but my intended audience was going to be looking at it for the entire length of the podcast, which is why I went for something more subdued.
