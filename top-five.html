Max's Top Five Movies

While this list may be subject to change in the future, these are currently my Top Five favorite movies.

  1. High Fidelity (2000) Legitmately one of the best RomComs of all time. Nothing comes close in my mind.
  2. Trainspotting (1196) A great movie about a heroin addict deciding it isn't for him anymore. Also his friends suck.
  3. Reservoir Dogs (1992) Around five or six dudes try and rob a bank and they fail miserably.
  4. The Big Lebowski (1998) An amazing movie about really nothing at all.
  5. Hot Fuzz (2007) A hilarious parody of cop/action movies. No matter how many times I rewatch it, I always catch something new every time.
